# title: Fire
# keywords: fire, smoke, flame, burning, blaze, house fire, escape
# sources: FEMA, NFPA, Ready.gov

▲ URGENT: Don't gather belongings — crawl low and get out of the building immediately, call 911/112 from outside.

» PROTOCOL:
01. Stay low and crawl; clean air is near the floor.
02. Before opening a door, test it with the back of your hand — if hot, DON'T open it, find another exit.
03. If clothes catch fire: STOP-DROP-ROLL; cover your face with your hands.
04. If trapped: seal door gaps with wet cloth.
05. Go to a window; wave a light-colored cloth and tap rhythmically to attract attention.
06. NEVER use elevators — stairs only.
07. Once out, do NOT go back inside; move to the assembly point.
08. Call 911/112 and report building number and floor.

◆ CRITICAL:
— 80% of fire deaths are caused by smoke inhalation, not flames.
— NEVER pour water on a grease fire — cover with a lid or turn off the stove.
— For electrical fires: cut the power first, then use a dry chemical extinguisher.
— Don't forget small children and pets, but don't endanger yourself searching for them.
