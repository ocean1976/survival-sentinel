# title: First Aid
# keywords: first aid, wound, bleeding, heart, cpr, choking, burn, injury, blood, broken
# sources: WHO, American Red Cross, CDC

▲ URGENT: Check consciousness and breathing first; if no breathing, call emergency services and start CPR immediately.

» PROTOCOL:
01. Assess consciousness: call out, tap shoulders gently.
02. Check breathing: watch for chest movement, listen for 10 seconds.
03. If no breathing, call 911/112, then start CPR.
04. CPR: press center of chest with both hands — 5-6 cm depth, 100-120 times per minute.
05. 30 compressions + 2 rescue breaths; if untrained, do chest compressions only.
06. For severe bleeding: press clean cloth directly on wound — hold for 10 minutes without lifting.
07. Elevate the injured limb above heart level if possible.
08. For burns: hold under cool running water for 10-20 minutes, cover with clean cloth.

◆ CRITICAL:
— Never give water to an unconscious person — choking risk.
— If spinal injury is suspected, do NOT move the person.
— Never apply oil, toothpaste, or ice on burns.
— For poisoning, call Poison Control before inducing vomiting.
