# title: Earthquake
# keywords: earthquake, quake, tremor, aftershock, rubble, seismic, shaking, collapsed
# sources: FEMA P-2064, Ready.gov, USGS

▲ URGENT: DROP-COVER-HOLD ON — get on your knees, take cover under a sturdy table, protect your head with both arms.

» PROTOCOL:
01. Get under a sturdy table or crouch in an interior wall corner.
02. Protect your head and neck with your arms.
03. Stay in position until shaking stops — if the table moves, move with it.
04. Stay away from glass, heavy furniture, and windows.
05. Once shaking stops, put on shoes and watch for glass and debris.
06. If you smell gas, turn off the valve and open windows.
07. NEVER use elevators — take the stairs.
08. Outside: stay away from buildings and power lines.

◆ CRITICAL:
— Doorframes are NOT safer (outdated advice); under a table or interior corner is better.
— Aftershocks are most dangerous in the first 72 hours; stay prepared.
— If trapped under rubble, don't shout — conserve energy, tap on pipes/walls in a rhythm.
— If near the coast, move to high ground immediately after shaking stops (tsunami risk).
