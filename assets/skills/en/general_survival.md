# title: General Survival
# keywords: survival, help, emergency, danger, disaster, crisis, what do i do, lost, stranded
# sources: FM 21-76, FEMA, Ready.gov

▲ URGENT: Stop, breathe, assess your situation. Panic is your biggest enemy — think before you act.

» PROTOCOL:
01. S.T.O.P. — Sit down, Think, Observe, Plan.
02. Address immediate threats: fire, flooding, structural collapse, severe bleeding.
03. Shelter: protect yourself from weather — find or build cover.
04. Water: you need water within 3 days. Collect rain, find streams, or purify.
05. Signal: make yourself visible — bright colors, mirrors, smoke, three of anything (fires, whistles, marks).
06. Stay put if rescuers know your location; move only if staying is dangerous.
07. Conserve energy and resources. Ration food and water.
08. Stay positive and focus on one task at a time.

◆ CRITICAL:
— Rule of 3: 3 minutes without air, 3 hours without shelter (extreme weather), 3 days without water, 3 weeks without food.
— Never drink seawater or urine — it accelerates dehydration.
— If injured, treat yourself first — you can't help others if you're incapacitated.
— Keep a routine to maintain mental resilience in prolonged crises.
